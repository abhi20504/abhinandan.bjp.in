About Me

I am Abhinandan Chatterjee, a student based in Kolkata, West Bengal. Member of the Bharatiya Janata Party. My interests include Indian history and civilisation, political thought, cultural traditions, governance and public affairs.

I believe that ideas become meaningful when they are translated into sustained work, capable institutions and constructive public engagement. This website serves as a central place to learn about my background, initiatives, views and activities.

My work is at an early stage. I intend to document its development honestly, adding verified milestones, publications and activities as they take place.
